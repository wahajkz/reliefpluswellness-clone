GOOD TO KNOW
Cancellation Policy

We kindly ask that patients provide at least
24 hours' notice if they need to cancel or
reschedule their appointment. This allows
us to offer the appointment time to other

patients who may be waiting.

To cancel or reschedule, please contact us
by phone at +1 (416) 461-1118 or email at
info@reliefpluswellness.com. We understand
that unexpected situations arise and
appreciate your consideration.
