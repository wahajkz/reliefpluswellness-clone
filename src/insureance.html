GOOD TO KNOW
What Is Direct
Billing?

Direct billing is a service that allows participating healthcare providers
to
submit eligible claims directly to your insurance company on your
behalf.

Depending on your insurance coverage
and plan details, some or all of
the treatment cost may be processed
through your insurer. Coverage,
reimbursement, and eligibility vary
by provider and policy.

HOW IT WORKS
Our Direct Billing Process
01

Provide Your Insurance
Information

Share your insurance details before or
during your appointment.

02

Verification

Our team will verify whether direct
billing is
available for your insurance
provider.

03

Claim Submission

Eligible claims may be submitted
directly to your insurance company.

04

Remaining Balance

If your plan does not cover the full
amount, you
may be responsible for
any remaining balance.
